*IUBAT Notes Platform*

A website where IUBAT students share their study notes with each other, and where faculty check the notes before everyone else sees them.

This is a Practicum project and it is still being built.

*The idea*
Some students make excellent notes, but those notes usually stay with a few friends. This platform gives them a proper place to share, and gives juniors a way to find notes that are actually good.

A student uploads notes for a course. The faculty for that course read them and approve them, send them back with feedback, or reject them. Once a note is approved, other students can read it, ask questions, leave comments and rate it. Over time, the most helpful notes rise to the top.

*Who uses it*
Students upload notes, read approved notes, comment and rate.
Faculty review the notes uploaded for their courses and give feedback.
Admin keeps everything running: checks new accounts, sorts out difficult cases and looks after the site.
